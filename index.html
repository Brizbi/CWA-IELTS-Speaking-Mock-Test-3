Test instructions
Please read the following instructions carefully before beginning the test.

Overview

  • This is the IELTS Speaking Test. The test is divided into 3 parts.

Part 1

  • You will be asked questions about yourself, your home, work or studies, and other familiar topics.
  • Please read each question aloud before giving your answer.
  • You will be given a total of 6 minutes to complete Part 1.
  • If you finish before the 6 minutes are up, please click the Next button to move to the next part.
Microphone access is required to record your responses.

Part 2 — Instructions
Long turn

What to expect

  • You will see a task card (cue card) with a specific topic and several prompt questions.
  • You have exactly 1 minute to prepare and make notes.
  • Please read the cue card aloud before giving your response.
  • This is followed by up to 4 minutes of speaking time.
Part 2 — Cue Card
4:00 Prep: 1:00
Part 2
Task card
 Please read the cue card aloud before giving your response.
Describe a piece of electronic equipment that you find useful.
You should say:
  • What it is
  • How you learned to use it
  • How long you have had it
And explain why you find this piece of electronic equipment useful.

Discussion questions

Technology and housework
  • Question 1
    What kinds of machine are used for housework in modern homes in your country?
  • Question 2
    How have these machines benefited people? Are there any negative effects of using them?
  • Question 3
    Do you think all new homes will be equipped with household machines in the future? Why?
Technology in the workplace
  • Question 4
    What kinds of equipment do most workers need to use in offices today?
  • Question 5
    How have developments in technology affected employment in your country?
  • Question 6
    Some people think that technology has brought more stress than benefits to employed people nowadays. Would you agree or disagree?
